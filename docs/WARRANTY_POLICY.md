# Warranty policy

The terms set by the owner. The live text is in `src/data/pages.js` (the
`/warranty`, `/returns` and `/shipping` pages and Terms section 24) and the
product-page line comes from `src/data/warranty.js`. Change the numbers in those
two files and in this table together.

## Terms

- **Frames: 5-year limited warranty.**
- **Everything else: 1-year limited warranty** (printed graphics and fabrics,
  signs, flags, table covers and runners, lanyards, wristbands, print
  collateral, channel letters, and parts other than the frame, including LEDs
  and electrical components).
- **No lifetime warranties.**
- **Print defects: free reprint, valid for 1 year from delivery.**
- **No refunds.** A confirmed problem is fixed by a reprint or a reship.
- **Transit damage: report within 3 days of delivery** and we reship.

## Not covered

- Wind, storm and weather damage.
- Misuse, accidents and normal wear and tear.
- Fading from long exposure to sunlight.
- Artwork mistakes approved on the proof.
- Changes or repairs made by anyone other than Apex.
- Installation and electrical hookup (illuminated signs must be connected by a
  licensed electrician).

## Open items

- Confirm with each supplier that they back a 5-year frame and a 1-year term on
  everything else; a warranty longer than the supplier's comes out of Apex's
  pocket.
- "No refunds" can conflict with consumer-protection rules and card-dispute
  rights in some US states and Canadian provinces. Have the final text reviewed.
- One refund remains in Terms: if Apex cancels an order because of its own
  pricing or description error, the customer is refunded.
