# Apex Trade Show — Digital Authority Plan

A legitimate, no-spam plan to build third-party authority and entity trust for
apextradeshow.com. **Never** buy links, use PBNs, fabricate press, or seed fake
Reddit/Quora/reviews. Every tactic below earns citations by being genuinely
useful or by real relationships.

## 1. Entity foundation (on-site — mostly done)
- Consistent descriptor everywhere: **"Apex Trade Show — Trade Show Displays & Event Branding."**
- Organization + WebSite schema with linked `@id` graph (implemented in `index.html`).
- Accurate `llms.txt`, `robots.txt`, split sitemaps, canonical product/category pages.
- **Owner action:** create and verify official social/profile pages, then add their
  URLs to `brand.js` `socialProfiles` so we can emit `sameAs` — this is the single
  biggest entity-disambiguation win (separates Apex from unrelated "APEX" events).

## 2. Owned linkable assets (build → earns natural links)
Priority order by likely citation value:
1. Free 10×10 Trade Show Booth Checklist (printable PDF + on-page).
2. Trade Show Booth Budget Calculator (interactive).
3. Canopy Size Calculator (10×10 vs 10×15 vs 10×20 by table count).
4. Artwork Resolution Calculator (upload dimensions → is it print-ready?).
5. Banner / Backdrop Size Guide (which size for a 10×10 booth).
6. Complete Event Branding Guide (long-form hub).

Tools must be genuinely useful, not gated lead-capture.

## 3. Real relationship-based outreach (earn mentions)
Target real, relevant publishers/organizations — pitch expertise, not link buys:
- Event-planning & trade-show publications and blogs.
- Sign / wide-format / print-industry publications.
- B2B and small-business marketing resources.
- Vendor / farmers-market / festival organizer guides.
- Chambers of commerce and local business associations.
- Exhibition & event-marketing podcasts (offer a guest on "booth on a budget").
- Design/branding publications (dye-sublimation, booth design).
- **Customers** (with permission): a "branded by Apex" credit/link from their site.
- Trade associations and event organizers Apex actually works with.

## 4. Real customer proof (highest trust, owner-gated)
- Collect written permission to feature customer booths (see `/customer-gallery` infra).
- Turn permissioned projects into case studies (`/case-studies` infra).
- Request reviews only through legitimate channels (verified customers, a real
  Google Business Profile if/when one exists). Never seed review schema.

## 5. Original data (later, when real volume exists)
Once enough anonymized real order/configuration data exists, publish honest reports
(most popular booth sizes, common canopy configurations, average booth budget,
common artwork problems). **Never invent data.**

## Explicitly forbidden
Purchased backlinks, PBNs, link exchanges at scale, fake customer articles, fake
Reddit/Quora/forum recommendations, fabricated press releases, spam directories,
fake comparison "reviews" against competitors, fake local presence.

## Suggested 90-day sequence
- **Weeks 1–3:** Ship the Booth Checklist + Canopy Size Calculator (assets that earn links). Add `sameAs` once socials exist.
- **Weeks 4–8:** Budget Calculator + Complete Event Branding Guide. Begin real outreach to 15–20 relevant publishers/organizers.
- **Weeks 9–12:** Stand up `/customer-gallery` + first case study from a permissioned customer. Set up a Google Business Profile decision (online-only businesses can still use a service-area profile — owner decision).
