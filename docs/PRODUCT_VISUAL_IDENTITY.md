# Product Visual Identity

What must (and must not) appear in each Apex product's commerce imagery. A card
or gallery image that violates this is a **product error**, even if attractive.
Enforced in part by `scripts/verify-media.mjs` (no cross-product primary reuse).

## Banner stands

| Product | Must show | Must NOT show |
|---|---|---|
| **Standard Retractable** | Full-height floor retractable, compact aluminium cassette, **two stabilising feet**, graphic pulls vertically, support pole | X-frame, corner tension rods, tabletop proportions, Deluxe chrome-end cassette |
| **Deluxe Retractable** | Full-height retractable, heavier premium cassette, **chrome-style end caps**, adjustable pole | Standard two-foot cassette as the hero, X-frame, tabletop hardware |
| **X-Stand** | Exposed **X-shaped rear frame**, banner tensioned from corners, grommets, lightweight | Roll-up cassette, retractable mechanism, tabletop unit |
| **Table Top** | **Mini** retractable, small horizontal cassette sitting on a table/counter, small scale | X-frame, corner rods, floor-standing feet, full-height proportions |

Standard and Deluxe must **not** share a hardware hero image.

## Table covers

| Product / option | Must show | Must NOT show |
|---|---|---|
| **Standard / draped** | Naturally draped throw, relaxed folds | Tension-fitted stretch look |
| **Stretch** | Fitted, tensioned, shaped around the table legs | Loose/draped throw |
| **3-sided (open back)** | Rear visibly **open** (legs/access) | A closed-back rear |
| **4-sided (closed back)** | Fabric covering **all four** sides incl. rear | An open rear |

## Backdrops

| Product | Must show | Must NOT show |
|---|---|---|
| **Step & Repeat** | Adjustable telescoping tube frame + repeating-logo fabric | SEG/tension-fabric/pop-up hardware |

## Canopies

| Product | Must show | Must NOT show |
|---|---|---|
| **10×10 / 10×15 / 10×20** | The **selected size** footprint and the **selected wall config** (no/full/half/mixed) | A different size's art; walls the buyer did not select |

Canopy previews are generated from the configurator selection (`TentGallery`),
so they track the chosen size + wall count rather than a static file.
